Indonesia's Dual Challenge

Every nation is striving for sustainability. For Indonesia, this journey is complicated by climate risks and inefficient tax collection.

The Tax Problem

Our tax-to-GDP ratio is one of the lowest in ASEAN. A key reason? Our land and property tax system still relies on conventional, often inaccurate, manual data.

This leads to property misvaluation, lost revenue, and an unfair system for everyone.

The Energy Problem

Indonesia's sunlight potential is immense. Billions of square meters of rooftops are waiting to be transformed into clean energy sources.

Yet, today, calculating this city-wide potential on a mass scale is incredibly difficult.

The GEOSPECTRA Solution

An intelligent AI platform that processes satellite imagery with deep learning to create detailed 3D building models

Dual Benefits for Sustainable Development
